How we work

We learn your process first.

Automating a messy process just makes the mess faster. So we understand it before we build it.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    You walk us through the work that eats your week. We ask about tools, volume and where things fall through.

  2. 02

    Scope and plan

    Build and test
